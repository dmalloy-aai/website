## Program benefits

**H2:** More than free credits

**Intro (right-aligned):** _Placeholder — lorem ipsum in design; final copy needed_

### Benefit cards (2 rows × 3)

**Credits across every product**
A fixed credit amount that works across AssemblyAI's voice AI infrastructure platform including Speech-to-text API, Streaming speech-to-text API, Dictation API, and more.

**Startup pricing that scales**
Startup discounts continue after your credits run out. Standard pricing phases in with no migration and no cliff, so graduating from the program is seamless.

**Hands-on product guidance**
Dedicated Forward Deployed Engineers from the AssemblyAI team work with you directly, matching the right models and products to your use case from first prototype to production.

**Apply in minutes**
A short application. Tell us what you are building, your stage, and who backs you. No decks required.

**Get a fast decision**
Applications are reviewed within one week. Portfolio companies of partner VCs, including Accel and Y Combinator are fast-tracked and should receive decisions in 48 hours.

**Build with our team**
Credits land in your console within 48 hours of approval and work across most AssemblyAI products. Your dedicated Forward Deployed Engineer will reach out in week one.
